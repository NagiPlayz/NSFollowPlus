# In-server acceptance checklist

Use two players (Alice and Bob) on a clean Paper/Purpur 1.20+ server. For full pagination, seed more follows with additional accounts or test on a populated server. Commands should return immediately even if the database/skin service is slow.

## Commands and messaging

- [ ] `/follow`, `/followplus` and `/nsfollow` open the same 27-slot `NSFollowPlus | Overview`. `/follow Alice` as Alice reports that self-following is forbidden.
- [ ] `/follow Bob` as Alice adds one follow, updates both counters, sends `&b&lFOLLOW &8‣ &aYou are now following &bBob&a!`, and notifies Bob if he accepts alerts. Running `/follow Bob` **again** only says Alice is already following Bob; it never removes the follow, changes counters, or opens a GUI.
- [ ] `/unfollow Bob` opens the existing Confirm/Cancel GUI immediately after an asynchronous database membership check. No relationship changes before Confirm. `/unfollow Cara` (not followed) reports `You are not following Cara.` and opens no GUI. Cancel leaves the relationship intact; Confirm sends the existing unfollow success text and returns to Alice's Following page 1. If the follow was removed in the meantime, Confirm reports not-following, not a false success.
- [ ] `/follow profile` opens your own Overview. `/follow profile Bob` opens Bob's `Profile | Bob` Overview with Bob's UUID/name on head 4 and his actual counts, with read-only privacy dye; click 11/13 to see Bob's lists. The dye must **never** modify Bob's or Alice's settings while Bob is the subject.
- [ ] While Alice views `Profile | Bob`, let Bob use `/follow toggle`, `/follow alert`, and his own dye. After each committed write Alice's **same open inventory** updates Bob's dye and both status lines, stays read-only, and still shows Bob's head/counts. Alice's own privacy flags must not change.
- [ ] `/followers`, `/following`, `/followers Bob` and `/following Bob` show lists for the correct subject. In Bob's Following list, all heads are informational, never show an Unfollow prompt, and cannot open a confirmation or modify Alice's relationships. Next/Previous retain Bob as subject; Back returns to `Profile | Bob`. Own Following heads still open confirmation normally.
- [ ] `/follow leaderboard` opens the leaderboard when enabled; `/follow top` and `/follow toggle on|off` / `/follow alert on|off` are invalid. Rank 1 and later ranks display the expected yellow rank and white names.
- [ ] With your Overview open, `/follow toggle` flips **only** Allow Followers and preserves Follow Alerts. Starting with both on, the item changes from **Lime** to **Yellow** without closing/reopening the menu; its lore now reads `Allow Followers: DISABLED`, `Follow Alerts: ENABLED`. Another player cannot newly follow a private profile, but an existing follower can still unfollow. Running `/follow toggle` again restores Lime and the two enabled lines.
- [ ] With your Overview open, `/follow alert` flips **only** Follow Alerts and preserves Allow Followers. Starting with both on, the item changes from **Lime** to **Yellow**, with `Allow Followers: ENABLED` and `Follow Alerts: DISABLED`; a second call restores Lime. When both flags are off, toggling either command produces Yellow, never Lime or Red by itself. No on/off arguments or completions are accepted.
- [ ] `/follow help` displays the full formatted command list, `nsfollowplus.use` and `nsfollowplus.admin`; non-admins can read help, but only admins see/execute `/follow reload` in completion. The console can use help and reload, but not player GUIs.
- [ ] `/follow` first-argument completion offers `profile`, `leaderboard`, `toggle`, `alert`, `help`, `reload` (admins only), and online player names **excluding yourself**. `/follow profile <Tab>`, `/followers <Tab>` and `/following <Tab>` suggest online names including yours. Extra arguments never offer unrelated suggestions.
- [ ] `/unfollow <Tab>` suggests **only currently followed names** (case-insensitive prefix filtering), including offline followed players; it never falls back to all online players. On a cold cache, wait for the asynchronous refresh and press Tab again. Immediately after Confirm or `/follow Bob`, verify the cache refreshes to exclude/include Bob; a storage reload clears stale suggestions.
- [ ] Invalid argument counts, malformed player names, unknown offline targets and non-admin reloads give configured usage/error messages without synchronous Mojang or database operations on the tick thread. Previously recorded offline names remain executable even though ordinary online-player tab completion does not list them.

## Inventories

- [ ] Overview: head **4**, Heart of the Sea **11**, Compass **13**, Nether Star **15**, Lime/Yellow/Red Dye **22**; layout and stats match the supplied specification, with the updated three-color privacy indicator and `NSFollowPlus` brand label. Click 4 to refresh.
- [ ] Click 11/13/15 for Followers/Following/Leaderboard. Each menu has **54 slots**, with black stained-glass panes **only** at border slots **0–8, 9, 17, 18, 26, 27, 35, 36, 44, 45–47, 51–53**. Up to **28 player heads** occupy **10–16, 19–25, 28–34, 37–43**; **unused center slots are completely empty (AIR)**, never glass or loading items. Footer slots **48–50** hold navigation items, not filler. Hover a filler: its black-formatted space should suppress the default `Black Stained Glass Pane` title, with no lore.
- [ ] Followers heads have aqua names and exactly: gold `Information:`, gray-dot status with green `Online`/red `Offline`, and `Follower of <your name>` with aqua viewer name.
- [ ] Following heads have aqua names, the same status line, an empty line, and a red `▸ Click to Unfollow`. Clicking a head (including center slot **43**) opens `Unfollow | <target>` **without changing either count or relationship** and without sending an unfollow message. The black border and empty AIR center cells remain inert.
- [ ] Confirmation is **27 slots**, titled `Unfollow | <target>`, with black stained-glass filler everywhere except Lime Dye **11**, target head **13**, and Red Dye **15**. Compare their green/yellow/red names and lore to the three screenshots. Head 13 is informational and must be `PLAYER_HEAD` immediately, with the target UUID/name on `SkullMeta`; its texture resolves asynchronously.
- [ ] Clicking dye **15** returns to the *same Following page*, with no database changes or success message. Clicking dye **11** once unfollows transactionally, updates both counters, shows `&b&lFOLLOW &8‣ &cYou are no longer following &b<name>&c.`, and refreshes/clamps Following to the original page. Rapid repeated confirm clicks and a stale selected target never cause a re-follow. If the DB operation fails, the player can retry or cancel.
- [ ] Confirmation fillers, skull 13, SHIFT/number-key/double-click, bottom inventory and drags cannot trigger confirm, cancel or item movement. Pressing Escape closes the dialog without changing the relationship. While a confirm is in flight, duplicate confirm/cancel clicks are ignored.
- [ ] Leaderboard heads use yellow rank/white name and `Dynamic`, `Information:`, aqua/gray follower/following labels and green counts. Confirm rank 1 is not bolded differently.
- [ ] Bottom controls: Arrow **48** (first-page warning), Barrier **49** (back to your Overview for your own lists, or to the viewed player's Profile for target lists), Arrow **50** (last-page warning or next page). At **28** members there is one page; at **29** there are two; the Top 100 occupies **four** pages (28/28/28/16). On page 4, verify the 12 unused center slots are AIR. Check page 2 and return navigation.
- [ ] Dye 22 is **Lime Dye** when both settings are enabled, **Yellow Dye** when exactly one is enabled, and **Red Dye** when both are disabled. The `★ PRIVACY SETTINGS` name remains yellow/bold. Its 11 lore lines are: dark-gray `NSFollowPlus`, blank, gold `Information:`, gray `Configure who can follow your profile`, gray `and toggle ` + green `join alerts` + gray `.`, blank, gold `Details:`, aqua `‣` + gray `Allow Followers: ` + that flag's green `ENABLED` or red `DISABLED`, aqua `‣` + gray `Follow Alerts: ` + its own exact status, blank, yellow `# Click to Toggle Settings!`. Check **both** Yellow combinations have distinct, correct lore. The equivalent `&#55FF55` (`&a`) and `&#FF5555` (`&c`) colors are in `menus.yml`.
- [ ] Standard **LEFT** and **RIGHT** clicks on Dye 22 follow this **four-step transaction**: `on/on → on/off → off/off → off/on → on/on`, giving `Lime → Yellow → Red → Yellow → Lime`. Check the database values and exact status line after **every** click, not merely the color. After a successful asynchronous commit, the open inventory redraws in place; `UI_BUTTON_CLICK` plays **once**, and **one** chat line is sent: `privacy-enabled` for both on, `privacy-disabled` for both off, or `privacy-partial` for either mixed state. No second alerts message is sent. A failed write plays no success sound and leaves the previous dye/lore displayed. Rapid repeat clicks while a write is pending must not queue another step.
- [ ] SHIFT_LEFT, SHIFT_RIGHT, number-key swap, double-click and other clicks on Dye 22 do not change either setting. Try clicks and drags across the GUI/player inventory: no menu item can be taken or moved. Use `/follow alert` to turn alerts off while follower permission remains on: the dye becomes **Yellow** and its two lines show `ENABLED`/`DISABLED`; the next ordinary dye click moves to **Red** with both off.
- [ ] Compare tooltip colors to the supplied screenshots: profile title yellow; `Information:` / `Details:` gold; follower accents aqua; following/online values green; disabled navigation title gray and warning red; barrier title red. The newer `NSFollowPlus` title, 54-slot lists, and the single yellow privacy instruction remain intentional.
- [ ] Open Overview, Leaderboard, Followers, Following and Unfollow Confirmation with uncached heads. Every populated profile slot (and Overview's loading head) is `PLAYER_HEAD` **immediately** with the intended player's UUID/name; none is paper. Once an asynchronous texture arrives, the same head gains that skin without losing its colored name/lore or click target. Reopen a menu: cached skins should appear immediately. Test an offline-mode UUID whose Mojang name has a skin (name fallback), and a nonexistent skin (still a player head with an unresolved/default appearance).
- [ ] Navigate/close rapidly during profile skin loading. No stale skull should appear in the wrong slot and there should be no async Bukkit API errors, synchronous name lookups or stalled server ticks.

## Persistence and alerts

- [ ] Start with an empty plugin data folder: `plugins/NSFollowPlus/` contains `config.yml`, `menus.yml`, `messages.yml`, and `database/`; `database/follow.db` exists and `follow.db`, `follow.db-wal`, `follow.db-shm` never appear at the plugin root. If journal files appear while running, they also reside in `database/`.
- [ ] Stop the server and back up the data folder; on a test installation, move an old `follow.db` (plus any journal files) back to the plugin root, remove `database/follow.db`, and restart. The existing data and settings must survive the automatic move into `database/`. Never copy only the main DB if a WAL exists.
- [ ] Restart the server: followers, following, both settings, usernames and counts remain intact.
- [ ] Follow an offline Bob. On Bob's next join, Bob sees the pending follow alert (if enabled). Unfollow before Bob joins and verify no stale notice.
- [ ] When Bob rejoins, Alice receives a join alert while online and alerts enabled; disable Alice's alerts and verify she does not receive it next time.
- [ ] After many follows, the Top 100 updates without the main thread performing a SQL or Mojang HTTP lookup.
- [ ] Edit `plugins/NSFollowPlus/messages.yml` (for example, change `prefix` and `follow-success`), run `/follow reload`, and verify `/follow Bob` uses the new text immediately; unrelated/missing keys use bundled defaults. Test `&`, `&#RRGGBB`, `<#RRGGBB>`, `<bold>` and `channel: ACTION_BAR`.
- [ ] As an op, run `/follow reload` and `/nsfollow reload`; the console can do the same. A non-admin is denied with `reload-no-permission` and never sees `reload` in tab completion. Config changes are live without restarting.
- [ ] When upgrading an existing data folder, check the old `PINK_DYE` in stored `menus.overview.items.privacy.states.disabled_material` **renders as Red** (runtime compatibility migration). Copy the bundled privacy block into on-disk `menus.yml` to make `mixed_material: YELLOW_DYE` and `disabled_material: RED_DYE` explicit; keep `LEFT`/`RIGHT: TOGGLE_PRIVACY`, remove any obsolete `SHIFT_RIGHT` action, then reload. Check on-disk YAML is not silently overwritten. On a disposable copy of `menus.yml`, set one paged menu's filler range back to `0-53` (legacy layout), and optionally add a `loading_item` at content slot 10. Reload and verify all unused content slots still stay AIR before and after loading; existing heads and navigation remain visible. Restore the border-only filler range afterward.
- [ ] Edit `menus.yml`: change the overview title using MiniMessage, move a button to another empty slot, change its material/model data/glow/click action, move navigation inside the inventory, change one paged menu's `content_slots` and filler color. Move confirmation's three items and change its title to include both `%player%` and `%target%`; run `/follow reload` while the confirmation is visible. Verify the target binding survives the reload and cancel still leaves the relationship intact. Also reload immediately after clicking Cancel: the confirmation must not reappear or allow a follow to be removed. Other menus retain their configured defaults. A hidden item (`visible: false`) must not act when clicked.
- [ ] Trigger disabled previous/next controls and confirm the `messages.yml` first/last-page warnings. Try all click variants on default privacy (only ordinary LEFT/RIGHT advance the four-state cycle); test a custom click action including `CLOSE`. Shift/number-key/drag must never move an item. Play-sound changes in `config.yml` should take effect on reload.
- [ ] Change `max_leaderboard_entries` to 7 (the SQL ranking and default overview label both update), then set `allow_self_view_in_leaderboard: false` and verify your own head is omitted while other rank numbers stay global. Disable `enable_leaderboard` and confirm `/follow leaderboard` and its menu button refuse access with `leaderboard-disabled`.
- [ ] Attempt a bad YAML or overlap a navigation slot with a content slot. Reload should report `reload-failed`, keep the old menu and feature settings unchanged, and log why. Correct the file and reload again.
- [ ] For a disposable test only: switch SQLite filenames or MySQL credentials via `config.yml` and `/follow reload`; verify the new connection works. Switching databases does **not** migrate existing follow data; back up/copy/migrate databases yourself first.
- [ ] Repeat on MySQL with a disposable database. Schema initialization during a storage reload is off-thread; follow/list commands should not block ticks.
